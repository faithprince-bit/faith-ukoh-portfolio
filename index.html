01 / ABOUT

Geospatial thinking for real-world environmental questions.

I combine geographic thinking with Earth observation, spatial analysis and clear visual communication to turn complex environmental datasets into useful evidence.

About me

From geography to geospatial insight.

I'm a Geography graduate (Remote Sensing & GIS) from the Federal University of Technology, Minna, where I focused on applying geospatial analysis to environmental and land-use challenges.

My work combines GIS, satellite imagery and spatial analysis to investigate environmental patterns, land-use dynamics and urban environmental challenges.

During my internship at NIHUB, I worked with cloud computing tools and cross-functional teams, which strengthened my approach to data-driven problem solving.

Outside technical work, I've spent over three years in elected student leadership roles, developing experience in coordination, teamwork, public speaking and organizing people.
